# Narration voices

The rule: video narration needs punch; a conversational voice (Tiffany Blake) suits feature walkthroughs, not launches or ads.

Verdicts from the first launch-video audition: Rachelle and Killian, liked, emotive (primary). Jesse and Asher, fine. Kristen, good voice but slow for this script. Cherry, too enthusiastic in places ("the brands that beat you"). Amelia, acceptable, not matched to the script. Rosie, monotonous.

A line that starts with a brand name that sounds like a question word is heard as a question in every voice, even with "!": put the name mid-sentence ("Meet <brand>.", "That's where <brand> comes in.") or voice it with the lines around it as context (ElevenLabs `previous_text`/`next_text`), and check the take.
